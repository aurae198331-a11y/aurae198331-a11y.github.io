About Aura

A complete event and exhibition company focused on creating meaningful, enjoyable and well-managed experiences.

Aura Events & Exhibitions is an event and exhibition company dedicated to bringing people, brands, businesses and communities together.

Our work includes cultural celebrations, Dandiya Nights, product exhibitions, jewellery, clothing, automobile exhibitions, concerts, corporate events and family celebrations.

We aim to make events simple, enjoyable and accessible for people from every background.

Our Vision

To create events where people can connect, celebrate, discover new products and create beautiful memories.
