About Aura

Events made simple, memorable and enjoyable for everyone.

We Create Experiences

Aura Events & Exhibitions is an event and exhibition organization focused on creating enjoyable and memorable experiences for people.

From colourful Dandiya Nights to exhibitions, concerts, corporate events and private celebrations, we bring people together and create moments worth remembering.

Our goal is simple — an event should be easy to understand, enjoyable to attend and memorable for everyone.

To create meaningful events and experiences where everyone feels welcome — whether it is a family, student, professional, business owner or visitor.


Our Approach

Simple planning. Good organization. Great experiences.
